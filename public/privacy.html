Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Mirza OS Content Empire is a content management platform designed to assist with creating, reviewing, managing and publishing original short-form digital content.

TikTok Integration

When a user chooses to connect their TikTok account, TikTok may provide Mirza OS Content Empire with information and permissions authorised by that user.

These permissions may be used to identify the connected account and to enable authorised content management and publishing functionality.

Information We Use

We only use information necessary to provide the features authorised by the user. We do not sell personal information.

Third-Party Services

Mirza OS Content Empire may integrate with third-party services such as TikTok. Use of those services is also subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authentication credentials and other information used by the platform.

Disconnecting TikTok

Users may revoke access to their TikTok account through applicable TikTok account settings or through available connection controls provided by the platform.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the platform and its integrations develop.
